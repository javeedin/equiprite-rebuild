Pick a unit from the queue

Each unit is checked against its class checklist, one question at a time. A failed question becomes a defect with its own photo. The result follows from the answers and defects:

  • Pass nothing failed: cleared to ship.
  • Conditional an optional check failed, a low rating, or a minor or cosmetic defect: ships once you acknowledge it.
  • Fail a required check failed, or a critical or major defect: the line is marked Inspection failed.

Check-out covers ship-out from the yard and from a site; check-in covers receipt at a site and back at the yard. Which classes are inspected where is the inspection matrix in Configuration.
