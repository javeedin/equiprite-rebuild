Stored statuses at this step, and how every screen shows them

Two stored statuses, one answer on screen

EquipRite stores a status on the request and one on each piece of equipment. Approving sets the request to IN_PROCESS, and it stays there while the equipment is picked, shipped, used and returned. On its own it says little: with one of three pieces shipped it still reads IN_PROCESS.

So every screen in EquipRite Next reads the request from its equipment. The request's own status answers only "is it approved?" (draft, waiting, sent back, cancelled); where it is comes from its pieces, with what is next, who does it, and what is late. The stored status stays one hover away.

Quick answers

What happens after I create a request?

You send it for approval. The Equipment Manager approves it (or sends it back with a reason, which you get), assigns a unit to each piece, and the yard picks, inspects when the class needs it, and ships it. You accept it on site, where it is billed and its time is entered. When the job is done you ask for the return; the yard receives it back, inspects it, and the request is closed. The diagram above shows each step and who takes it.

Can I approve from the Requests list?

Yes, with full access to Approve & allocate (an administrator or Equipment Manager). A request waiting for approval shows an Approve button on its row: it opens a small window where you approve every piece still waiting, or send it back with a reason. Afterwards it offers the next step: Assign units or Pick.

Why are approving and picking separate steps?

They are taken by different people and at different places: approving is a decision on the request; picking is the yard taking the assigned units off the shelf, and it starts the Fusion transfer order. Between them each piece needs a unit assigned. When you hold both roles, Requests puts them one after another: approve on the row, then the same window offers Pick as soon as units are assigned, and the row shows a Pick button while pieces wait at the yard.

Where do I see everything that happened to a request?

The clock beside its number in Requests opens its timeline: each step with when it was reached and by whom (approval included), and everything recorded, newest first. A request's own tab shows the same under History, and both export.

Why does a step say "the date was not recorded"?

The step was reached (its equipment is past it) but the server kept no status change for it. Dates come from the status history the server records, and from the transfer orders (raised, shipped, received). A step is never given a date it does not have.

Which steps still open New EquipRite?

Steps that write to Oracle Fusion: releasing a transfer order for picking, ship confirm of transfer-order pieces, receipts, cost postings and agreements' contracts. EquipRite Next shows the step and what it would do, and links to the same screen in New EquipRite, with the same data.
