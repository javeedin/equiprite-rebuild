Pick a unit from the queue

Each unit is checked against its class checklist. A failed question gets a note and becomes a defect with its own photo. The result follows from the answers and the defects:

  • Pass nothing failed: cleared to ship, or received in good order.
  • Conditional an optional check failed, a low rating, or a minor or cosmetic defect: it goes once you acknowledge the remarks.
  • Fail a required check failed, or a critical or major defect: at the yard the line is marked Failed inspection and cannot ship.
