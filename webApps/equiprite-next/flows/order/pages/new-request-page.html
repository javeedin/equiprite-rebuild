A customer request is raised in New EquipRite for now

It needs more than a project request, and one of its steps writes to Oracle Fusion, which EquipRite Next does not do yet. In New EquipRite it takes:

  1. The customer and their contactfrom EquipRite's customer list, instead of a project and task
  2. The equipment, dates and delivery addressthe same catalog, at the customer's rates
  3. The rental termsaccepted before it is sent
  4. A Fusion sell contract

Once sent it goes straight to the Equipment Manager (a customer request has no project approver). What you picked here does not carry over.
